Community guidelines

Keep every CampusSwap exchange safe, honest, and respectful.

1
Describe items honestly

Use your own photos and clearly mention damage, defects, or missing parts.

2
Meet safely on campus

Choose a public, well-lit campus area and tell a friend where you are going.

3
Inspect before completing

Check the item carefully before paying and marking it as collected.

4
Protect personal information

Never share passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary banking information.

5
Be respectful

No harassment, scams, prohibited items, spam, or pressure to complete a swap.

6
Report suspicious listings

Use the report option when a listing or student behaviour feels unsafe.

Delete your account?

Your profile will disappear and you will be logged out. Your completed history and seller reviews will remain archived so they can be restored if you register again.
