# 🎓 CampusSwap

**CampusSwap** is a responsive web-based student marketplace prototype designed to make buying, selling, and exchanging second-hand items within a university community simpler, safer, and more convenient.

The system was developed as part of a **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** project, with a focus on usability, responsive design, intuitive navigation, and the specific needs of university students.

---

## 💡 The Idea

Students often rely on general marketplace platforms, social media, or group chats to buy and sell second-hand items.

CampusSwap explores what a marketplace designed specifically for a university community could look like — bringing item discovery, communication, reviews, and moderation into one platform.

---

## 🎙️ Meet Ora

**Ora** is CampusSwap's marketplace assistant, designed to make finding items quicker and more natural.

Instead of manually searching through listings, users can use voice-based queries such as:

> "Find me the cheapest calculator."

Ora interprets the request and helps identify suitable listings available within the marketplace.
